v0.3.0-alpha — three of four self-hosting stages

Strata's lexer, parser and type checker are written in Strata: 2,313
lines, 4% native C. Each is proven against its Python counterpart by a
differential test running in CI — byte-identical token streams,
identical syntax trees, identical diagnostics — across the corpus and on
their own source.

Self-hosting is NOT complete. codegen.sta and the stage1/stage2 fixpoint
remain.

The language gained the floor it was missing: loops, assignment and
indexing. It previously could not express a lexer.

Verification replaced assertion. The conformance suite reported "32/32"
while failing 30/31 with a segfault; it is now 63 tests and green. CI
never ran the suite; it now runs six, under both gcc and clang.
Documentation is compiled on every commit.

Twelve of fifteen tools in bin/ printed success without doing the work
they claimed and are quarantined. Unmeasured performance figures are
removed. See CHANGELOG.md for the full list.
